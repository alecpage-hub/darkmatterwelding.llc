OUR STORY

About Dark Matter Welding

Hi, I'm Alec Page, founder of Dark Matter Welding. A year ago, I started this company with my best friend, Martin Rivas, and it has been an incredible journey.

Martin and I met in welding school, where we discovered we had more than just a passion for welding in common—we built a genuine friendship. Through long days in the shop, hard work, and plenty of learning experiences, we realized we shared the same vision: creating high-quality work while treating every customer with honesty and respect.

Dark Matter Welding was born from a simple idea: provide exceptional welding and fabrication services while making the experience easy, dependable, and stress-free for our customers. We take pride in our craftsmanship, attention to detail, and commitment to delivering results that stand the test of time.
